Privacy Policy for My Habits

Effective date: 2026-10-13

1. Who is responsible

My Habits (Spanish name: Mis hábitos) is a free Android app created by tucutucuapps ("the developer"). For any privacy question write to tucutucuapps@gmail.com.

2. Summary

  • You can use the whole app without creating an account. In that case your habits are stored only on your phone.
  • If you choose to sign in with Google, your habits are copied to the cloud (Firebase, by Google) to back them up and sync them between your phones.
  • The app shows a Google AdMob banner ad. Before showing ads we ask for your consent where the law requires it.
  • We do not sell your data. We do not use our own usage analytics or crash reporting tools.

3. Data stored on your phone

The app stores the following on your phone, without sending it to any server of ours:

  • Your habits: name, color, icon, scheduled weekdays, order and creation date.
  • The record of the days you marked as done.
  • The celebrations already shown (so they are not repeated).
  • Your preferences, such as reminder settings and whether you have already seen the welcome screen.

The current streak and best streak are calculated on your phone and are not stored separately.

Android may include this data in your Google account's automatic backup (the system backup feature). Google manages that backup according to your Android settings, not the app.

4. Data uploaded to the cloud (only if you sign in with Google)

If you choose "Continue with Google", the app uses Google Sign-In, Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore (Google services) and stores in your account:

  • The data Google shares when you sign in: account identifier, your name, your email address and your profile photo.
  • The same habit data that is on your phone (section 3): habits, completed days, celebrations and each habit's reminder.

What it is used for: backing up your habits and keeping them up to date across your phones. It is not used for advertising and it is not sold.

Each account can only read and write its own data (Firestore security rules). Data travels encrypted (HTTPS/TLS). Google, as the Firebase provider, also processes technical data needed to run the service, such as the IP address, under its privacy policy and the Firebase privacy terms.

If you do not sign in, nothing is uploaded to the cloud.

5. Advertising (Google AdMob) and consent

My Habits is free and is funded by a Google AdMob banner shown on the List and on each habit's Detail screen. To show it, AdMob and Google may collect and use, depending on your consent and your region:

  • Your device's advertising ID.
  • Device and app data (model, Android version, language, app version).
  • The IP address (from which an approximate location is derived) and data about how you interact with ads.

This data is used to show ads, measure their performance and prevent fraud. If you do not give consent where the law requires it (for example, in the European Economic Area), non-personalized ads are shown.

Consent is handled with Google's form (UMP, User Messaging Platform) the first time you open the app. Where applicable, you can change your decision at any time with the "Ad privacy" link at the bottom of the List.

You can also reset or disable the advertising ID in Android settings (Google › Ads).

More information: How Google uses data from sites or apps that use its services and Google's privacy policy.

6. Reminders

Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your phone. They use no servers and send no data off the device. To show them, Android 13 and above asks for the notification permission; you can deny or remove it at any time in the system settings. The app also uses that permission to reschedule reminders after the phone restarts.

7. Widgets

Home screen widgets show the habits you choose and let you check off today. They read the data stored on your phone and send nothing to any server.

8. How to delete your account and data

From the app:

  1. Open Settings and go to Account.
  2. Tap Delete account and cloud data.
  3. Confirm with Delete forever. Google may ask you to confirm your account.

Your account and the habits saved in the cloud are deleted. The habits on your phone are kept and the app keeps working without an account.

By email: write to tucutucuapps@gmail.com from the email of your Google account asking for deletion. We handle it within 30 days at most. More details on the account deletion page.

Data on your phone: to delete what is on your phone, delete the habit in the app, uninstall the app, or use "Clear data" in Android settings.

Advertising data: it is managed by Google. You can control it from your Google account and Android's ad settings.

9. Children

My Habits is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from children under that age. If you believe a child gave us data, write to us and we will delete it.

10. Your rights

Depending on your country, you may request access, correction or deletion of your data and withdraw your consent. Write to the contact email. For data held by Google (AdMob, Firebase) you can also exercise your rights with Google.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will publish the new version at this same address with its effective date. If the change is significant, we will also tell you in the app.

12. Contact
